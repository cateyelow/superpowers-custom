DEFECT 1
what: The step-indicator connector line is drawn on top of the "Shipping" and "Payment" labels, so both look struck through.
where: checkout.html:87-96 — `.step-indicator li:not(:last-child)::after` (top:16px; left:34px; z-index:0), painted over `.step-label`
repro: Isolated headless Chromium @1280. getBoundingClientRect: li#1 y=177.52 h=32, so the ::after band sits at y=193.52-195.52; the "Shipping" label box is x=389..448, y=183.02..204.02 — the 2px line crosses the text's vertical centre and its full width. category: responsive

That outline composites over the white card to rgb(214,221,247) = 1.35:1 against #ffffff, vs the 3:1 WCAG 1.4.11 minimum. Element screenshot of the focused Next button confirms the ring is barely distinguishable from the card. (Text inputs are fine — their :focus border really does turn #3157d5, 6.08:1.)
severity: blocking
category: a11y
