DEFECT 1
what: Keyboard focus ring on every button is almost invisible, so a keyboard user cannot see which button is focused (on steps 2 and 3 that means Back vs Next/Place order are indistinguishable).
where: checkout.html:207-210 `button:focus-visible { outline: 3px solid var(--focus) }` with `--focus: rgba(49, 87, 213, 0.2)` (checkout.html:18)
repro: Loaded the page at 1280x900, pressed Tab 6 times to reach `section[data-step="0"] .button-primary` (verified `:focus-visible` = true), screenshotted a 14px margin around the button, and diffed it pixel-for-pixel against the same crop with nothing focused. Exactly 3 pixel columns change, from rgb(255,255,255) to rgb(214,221,247) — contrast 1.35:1 against the adjacent white card, versus the 3:1 required for a focus indicator. Same measurement on the step-2 `.button-secondary` (Back): ring rgb(214,221,247), 1.35:1.
severity: blocking
category: contrast
