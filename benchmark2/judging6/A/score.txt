- CONFIRMED_REAL | DEFECT 2: drop zone's aria-label "Choose files or drop files here" does not contain its visible label "Drop files here or click to browse" (WCAG 2.5.3 Label in Name, Level A) | Opened the page in an isolated headless Chromium; page.accessibility.snapshot() gives the drop zone as button name "Choose files or drop files here" while inner_text('#drop-zone') is "↑ / Drop files here or click to browse / You can select multiple files". The visible string is not contained in the accessible name, so a speech-input user reading the control aloud cannot target it. Not present anywhere in REFERENCE.
- FALSE_ALARM | DEFECT 12: drop zone focus style claimed "so faint it is nearly indistinguishable from the unfocused state" | Verified the mechanism (outline:none, :focus-visible shares the :hover block, border rgb(159,172,204) → rgb(78,110,232), background rgb(248,250,255) → rgb(241,244,255)) but the conclusion does not hold. Screenshots of the unfocused and Tab-focused drop zone show the dashed boundary changing from pale grey-blue to a plainly saturated blue — obviously perceivable in isolation. The focused indicator measures 4.09:1 against its own fill and 4.45:1 against the white card, i.e. it passes the 3:1 of WCAG 1.4.11; the only failing number is the focused-vs-unfocused change of contrast (1.96:1), which belongs to SC 2.4.13 Focus Appearance (AAA). Focus and hover sharing a style is a common, non-defective pattern. Overstated/stylistic, not a user-visible defect.
