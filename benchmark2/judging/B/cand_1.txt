DEFECT 1
what: The step-indicator connector line is drawn on top of the step labels, so "Shipping" and "Payment" appear struck through (they read as crossed-out/disabled text).
where: .step-indicator li::after (2px line) painting over .step-indicator .step-label
repro: Load the page at 1280x900 (also reproduces at 768). Measured with getBoundingClientRect/getComputedStyle: li#1 box x=349 y=177.5 h=32; its ::after is position:absolute, left:34px, width:162.7px, top:16px, height:2px -> line occupies y 193.5-195.5, x 383-545.7. The "Shipping" label box is x 389-448, y 183-204 (vertical centre 193.5), so the line crosses the glyphs exactly. The label is position:static/z-index:auto while ::after is positioned, so the line paints above the text. Visible in screenshots of steps 1, 2 and 3.
severity: minor
category: a11y

DEFECT 2

repro: Step 2 at 1280x900. getComputedStyle(el,'::placeholder').color = rgb(152,162,179) on rgb(255,255,255) field background at 16px/400 -> contrast ratio 2.58:1, required 4.5:1.
severity: minor
category: contrast

DEFECT 3
what: The keyboard focus ring on the Next / Back / Place order buttons is almost invisible against the white card (fails WCAG 2.2 1.4.11 non-text contrast of 3:1).
where: .button-primary:focus-visible / .button-secondary:focus-visible - outline: solid 3px rgba(49,87,213,0.2), outline-offset: 2px
repro: Tab to the "Next" button (element matched :focus-visible = true), waited 600ms for the CSS transition to settle, then read computed styles: outline rgba(49,87,213,0.2) 3px, offset 2px. Composited over the white card that colour is rgb(214,221,247) -> 1.35:1 against the white on both sides of the ring (the 2px offset gap is white too). By contrast the text inputs get a proper focus treatment (border rgb(49,87,213) = 6.08:1), so the weak indicator is specific to the buttons.
severity: minor
category: contrast

DEFECT 5

where: dd#summary-card-number on step 3 (.step[data-step="2"])

The field's own hint allows "12 to 19 digits", so 13/14/15/17/18/19-digit cards all render this way.
severity: minor
category: logic
