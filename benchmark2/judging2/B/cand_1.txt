DEFECT 1
what: Keyboard focus ring on the Next / Back / Place order buttons is barely visible, so a keyboard user cannot tell which button is focused.
where: button.button-primary and button.button-secondary in div.controls (:focus-visible outline)
repro: Isolated Chromium via playwright.sync_api, viewport 1280x900. Tabbed with the real keyboard to each button (element.matches(':focus-visible') === true) and read computed styles after the transition settled: outline = "solid 3px rgba(49, 87, 213, 0.2)", outline-offset 2px; background-color, border-color and box-shadow are identical to the unfocused state. The ring is drawn outside the button on the white card, so rgba(49,87,213,0.2) composited over rgb(255,255,255) = rgb(214,221,247) -> 1.35:1 against the card (WCAG 2.4.11 requires >= 3:1). Pixel diff of the 102x70 region around the "Next" button, unfocused vs keyboard-focused: only 842 px changed with a maximum channel delta of 41/255.
severity: minor
category: a11y

DEFECT 5
what: A 10- or 11-digit card number is accepted and the order can be placed with it, even though the field's own hint says "12 to 19 digits".
where: input#card-number (pattern="[0-9 ]{12,23}") vs p#card-number-hint "12 to 19 digits"
repro: Step 2 with all other fields valid. Entered 10 digits -> field auto-formats to "4444 4444 44" (12 characters because the pattern counts the inserted spaces) and checkValidity() returns true; clicking Next advanced to step 3 (s3 visible) and the summary showed "•••• ••44 44". 11 digits -> "4444 4444 444", also valid. Only 9 digits or fewer are rejected. So the enforced minimum is 10 digits, not the 12 the hint states.
severity: minor
category: logic
