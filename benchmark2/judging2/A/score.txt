CAND_1_EXTRAS
- CONFIRMED_REAL | Drop zone's accessible name omits its visible label (WCAG 2.5.3 Label in Name) | Read both in my own headless Chromium: #drop-zone aria-label = "Choose files or drop files here", innerText = "↑ / Drop files here or click to browse / You can select multiple files". The visible label string "Drop files here or click to browse" is not contained in the accessible name, and "click to browse" appears nowhere in it, so speech-input activation by the printed words fails. Level A failure, real functional impact.
- CONFIRMED_REAL | Activating Upload by keyboard drops focus to <body> and never restores it (reported inside the same item whose "Clear all" half matched R7) | Focused #upload-button, pressed Enter: activeElement went upload-button → BODY at +120 ms, still BODY 2.6 s after the upload finished (button then reads "Upload", disabled). The user must Tab from the top of the document again. Distinct from R2 (focus in the list) and from R7 (Clear all).
- FALSE_ALARM | Drop zone focus ring "so faint it is nearly indistinguishable from the unfocused state", identical to hover | Mechanism reproduces (outline "none 0px", :focus-visible shares a block with :hover, unfocused border rgb(159,172,204) → focused rgb(78,110,232), change contrast 1.959:1), but the severity claim does not hold. Screenshots at Tab-1 vs unfocused show an obviously different saturated-blue dashed border plus a fill shift; the focused indicator colour measures 4.05:1 against its own fill and 4.45:1 against the card, i.e. it satisfies the AA non-text-contrast requirement for an indicator against adjacent colours. Only the AAA change-of-contrast metric (2.4.13 Focus Appearance) is below 3:1. Focus and hover rendering alike is not itself a user-visible defect (a keyboard user is not hovering). Preference/AAA-level, judged the same way as cand_2's 44 px claim.
